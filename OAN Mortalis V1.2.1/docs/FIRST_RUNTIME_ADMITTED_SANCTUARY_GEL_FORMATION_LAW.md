# FIRST_RUNTIME_ADMITTED_SANCTUARY_GEL_FORMATION_LAW

## Purpose

This note defines the first runtime-admitted formation seam for
`Sanctuary.GEL` in `V1.2.1`.

It exists to keep `.GEL` truthful as the first local formed product of the
tripartite rather than collapsing it into source authority, symbolic law, or
public derivative surfaces.

## Governing Compression

> The tripartite is `RootAtlas -> symbolic law body -> engrammitization`.
> `.GEL` is the first local formed product body of that tripartite.
> `SLI.Lisp` is the cryptic working medium in which that process is used; it is
> not the tripartite itself and not source sovereignty.
> The first lawful `.GEL` body is a `RuntimeAdmittedSubstrate`, never raw
> `RootAtlas`.
> The immediate upstream body for first `.GEL` formation is the bounded
> predicate pool derived from the formation data pool, not assent footing or
> regional package presence alone.
> First `.GEL` is formed under represented standing, not merely from available
> install materials.
> `.GEL` inclusion does not equal final `Sanctuary.GEL` survivor admission.

## Tripartite Office

The tripartite for first `.GEL` formation is:

1. `RootAtlas`
   - research-born symbolic predicate authority
   - remote source ancestry
   - upstream of local install
2. base language groupoid, transformer laws, and order-of-operations body
   - lawful symbolic transform discipline
   - including the bounded `IUTT`-like interval and operation law already fixed
     in this line
3. engrammitization
   - the actualization process that passes bounded transformed material into
     engram form

This tripartite produces `.GEL` for an environment.

`.GEL` is not one member of the tripartite.

## Product Office

The first runtime-admitted `Sanctuary.GEL` body is:

- one local admitted substrate record
- one identity-bearing formed product of tripartite passage
- one retained, rest-capable local body
- one bounded environment-local seat

It is not:

- local `RootAtlas`
- raw symbolic law
- the full semantic universe engine
- public-readable derivative output

## Working Medium Boundary

`SLI.Lisp` is the cryptic working medium where bounded symbolic law and
engrammitization are used.

It is not:

- the tripartite itself
- remote source authority
- local `.GEL` product body

Return to `Prime` and later human-facing products are downstream of formed
`.GEL` and are not part of first formation.

## First Identity Basis

The first local `.GEL` body should carry at minimum:

- admitted regional package basis
- predicate pool handle
- predicate family-bearing footing
- derived payload lineage
- local substrate handle
- formation receipt handle
- bounded symbolic anchor summary derived downstream of `RootAtlas`

This basis may preserve bounded lineage and anchor truth.

It may not imply:

- direct `RootAtlas` sovereignty
- Atlas mutation
- lab-side templating authority
- exposed validator posture

In the current phase, first `.GEL` formation consumes admitted English
regional package basis rather than abstract universal Atlas authority.

The localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation boundary now adds the surrounding
standing requirement: National, Regional, and Local standing must be
represented before first `.GEL` formation can be read as a localized
continuity-substrate event.

The `Generative Engrammitization Library` formation readiness boundary now
adds the upstream library-readiness distinction: rooted, witnessed,
`SLI`-formed, Engrammitization-facing candidates may be held in `.GEL`
readiness, but that inclusion is not localized formation, survivor admission,
first-use eligibility, governing `CME`, `RTME`, or runtime authority.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization boundary now adds the structured prior
distinction: UTF-8 witness, RootAtlas root predicate, attachment-native `SLI`
constructor, invariants, mutation-policy posture, transport-receipt posture,
and candidate-only admission ceiling may be recorded as a bounded library
prior without authorizing EC mutation, `SLI.Lisp`, transport, survivor
admission, or runtime.

## First State Ladder

The first lawful state ladder is:

- `Hydrated`
- `Admitted`
- `Engrammatized`
- `Retained`
- `Resting`
- `Refused`

These are formation and retention states only.

They do not grant:

- autonomous mutation
- predicate promotion
- continuity widening
- public projection

## Non-Collapse Rules

- no first `.GEL` body may claim local `RootAtlas` residency
- no first `.GEL` body may bypass symbolic transform witness
- no first `.GEL` body may bypass engrammitization witness
- no first `.GEL` body may widen into public derivative generation during
  formation
- no first `.GEL` body may imply return-to-Prime merely because formation
  succeeded

## Passive Formation Consequence

The first passive formation family may:

- receive bounded predicate pool basis derived from the formation data pool,
  admitted regional package basis, and derived formation basis
- witness inherited posture, trust, evidence, and response predicate families
- assess whether the tripartite witness is sufficient
- emit refusal where source authority, transform law, or engrammitization
  witness is missing or widened
- emit a first retained/rest-capable `.GEL` substrate record when the bounded
  derived basis is sufficient

It may not yet:

- perform live runtime hydration
- perform persistence IO
- emit public derivatives
- authorize later return-to-Prime or human-facing product work
- claim first use
- claim governing `CME`
- claim counsel-reviewed disclosure
- authorize domains

If the upstream predicate pool yields `Silence`, first `.GEL` formation must
not widen that posture into a rich refusal surface.

Formation answers whether the bounded local substrate exists.
Standing answers what the formed substrate is allowed to mean.

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now names the first lawful `.GEL` formation seam.

That seam treats `Sanctuary.GEL` as the first runtime-admitted local product of
`RootAtlas`, symbolic transform law, and engrammitization without collapsing
source authority, cryptic working form, and local substrate into one body, and
it now expects bounded predicate pool basis derived from the formation data
pool, including admitted English regional package footing, before local
formation.

The localized formation boundary contextualizes this seam without replacing
it: first `.GEL` formation is not automatic install output and remains below
Sanctuary pre-governing standing, first-use eligibility, governing `CME`,
`RTME`, and runtime authority.

The `GEL` formation readiness boundary also contextualizes this seam without
replacing it: `GEL = Generative Engrammitization Library`, and readiness is a
library-level candidate posture before localized standing and survivor
admission decide what the candidate may become.

The predicate-prior formalization boundary contextualizes this seam without
replacing it: `.GEL` may carry formal predicate priors, but those priors remain
candidate-only and do not become final `Sanctuary.GEL` survivor admission.
