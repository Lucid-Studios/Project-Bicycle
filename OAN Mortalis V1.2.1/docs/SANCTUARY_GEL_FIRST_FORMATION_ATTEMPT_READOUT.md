# SANCTUARY_GEL_FIRST_FORMATION_ATTEMPT_READOUT

## Purpose

This readout consolidates what the first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation attempt can
report in `V1.2.1`.

It adds no new authority, contract, reference data, service, evaluator,
runtime, admission gate, model-selection surface, or Cradle.GEL generation
path.

It exists to keep formation-attempt coherence legible before the line climbs
toward either first-use admission/enactment or Sanctuary.Actual approach work.

## Governing Compression

> First `Sanctuary.GEL` formation attempt may report coherence across seated
> prerequisite bodies; it does not convert coherence into survivor admission,
> first-use admission, Sanctuary.Actual governance, runtime activation, or
> Cradle.GEL generation.

## Seated Chain

The readout preserves this seated chain:

`GEL readiness -> GEL predicate prior -> localized pre-certification data pool -> localized formation floor -> Sanctuary.GEL regional substrate -> pre-governing standing -> first-use eligibility consideration -> first Sanctuary.GEL formation attempt`

`GEL = Generative Engrammitization Library` readiness supplies the
library-level candidate field.

`.GEL` predicate-prior formalization supplies structured candidate interiors.

The localized pre-certification data pool supplies represented source,
template, and standing inputs without activation.

The localized formation floor supplies represented National, Regional, and
Local standing.

`Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation names the bounded regional/root
local substrate required before later Sanctuary.Actual and Cradle.GEL work may
be considered.

Sanctuary pre-governing standing defines what the localized substrate may mean
before governance.

First-use eligibility consideration defines represented preconditions for
considering first use without admitting or enacting first use.

The first formation attempt asks whether those seated bodies cohere into one
receipted attempt.

## Disposition Readout

`Ready` means the attempt coheres as an attempt only.

`Held` means the attempt has recognizable represented structure, but one or
more local, domain, Special Case, counsel, regional, or governance questions
remain held.

`Refused` means a missing prerequisite or overclaim prevents the attempt from
standing.

No disposition admits survivor standing, grants first use, selects a model,
activates runtime, executes `SLI.Lisp`, invokes `RTME`, forms
Sanctuary.Actual, or generates Cradle.GEL.

## First-Use Split And Withheld Horizons

The first-use admission and enactment split is the next passive seam above this
readout.

That split distinguishes first-use admission from first-use enactment. It must
preserve that first-use eligibility is consideration only and that a formation
attempt is not first-use admission.

The governance approach path remains withheld.

That path may later define how a standing `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate
approaches Sanctuary.Actual Mother/Father governing `CME` formation. It must
preserve that approach is not formation and that a formation attempt is not
Sanctuary.Actual.

The recommended next seam after this readout is the first-use
admission/enactment split, now seated separately as a passive boundary.

This readout itself does not add that seam.

## Working Summary

The first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation attempt is now readable as a coherence
assessment, not an authority grant.

It may report whether seated prerequisite bodies cohere, remain held, or are
refused.

It does not promote that report into survivor admission, first-use admission,
Sanctuary.Actual governance, runtime activation, `SLI.Lisp`, `RTME`, model
selection, or Cradle.GEL generation.
