# Civic Pedagogical Skill Reconstruction Tree

Status: review-only corpus seed.  
Claim ceiling: educational manifold planning.  
Activation status: non-executive, non-credentialing, non-authorizing.

This document defines a first reconstruction-tree body for moving from not-knowing into skill, training, job duty, role readiness, and cross-domain transfer. It extends the `CIVIC_EDUCATION_DOMAIN_JOB_TEMPLATE_MATRIX.md` body into a pedagogical manifold without granting practice authority, employment authority, licensed authority, public authority, military authority, or Special Case authority.

## Tree Law

A learner does not carry a job title across universes. A learner carries witnessed invariants of capability.

A path may guide learning. A path may not certify authority.

A training packet may prepare practice. A training packet may not become a license.

An evidence leaf may show growth. An evidence leaf may not become office, command, or continuity admission.

The seed may transfer. The warrant does not.

## IUTT-Inspired Boundary

This tree uses IUTT-inspired language as methodological framing only. It does not import literal IUTT machinery, completed transport mathematics, proof-bearing reconstruction geometry, or formal equivalence across local worlds.

The working discipline is bounded:

1. Identify a local world.
2. Name the role being reconstructed.
3. Select invariants that must remain present.
4. Gather evidence leaves.
5. Assess whether role-readiness fruit is emerging.
6. Preserve seeds for transfer without claiming they work unchanged elsewhere.

| Tree Body | Meaning |

## Pedagogical Trunk

| Stage | Aim | Practices | Evidence Leaves | Refusal Locks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. | 1. | 2. Practice | Convert orientation into repeatable habits. | Apprenticeship; guided practice; simulation; scenario rehearsal; corrective feedback. | Completed mock task; feedback record; practice log; corrected mistake inventory. | No unsupervised live action beyond readiness; no impersonating public authority; no conflict escalation as substitute for skill. |
| 3. ## Transfer Invariants

Transfer is not identity preservation. Transfer is reconstruction under a new local world. A skill is a strong transfer candidate when this operational shape survives:

```text
notice -> order -> act -> record -> verify -> hand off
```

| Invariant | What Must Survive | Evidence Body |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | Noticing state, change, risk, and context. | Observation notes; before/after state; timestamped signal; relevance statement. |
| Sequencing | Ordering steps and dependencies. | Procedure trace; dependency list; checklist; failure mode if skipped. |
| Documentation | Making action legible. | Who, what, when, why; version; responsible party; retained artifact. |
| Safety Awareness | Seeing harm pathways before action. | Risk assessment; mitigation; boundary conditions; incident or near-miss log. |
| Repair | Diagnosing breakage and restoring function without erasing evidence. | Problem statement; cause hypothesis; repair action; verification. |
| Communication | Carrying information across roles without losing intent. | Audience; requested decision or action; readback; acknowledgment. |
| Measurement | Turning judgment into comparable signals. | Units; method; baseline; tolerance; calibration source. |
| Custody | Preserving control, provenance, and authorized transfer. | Chain; access log; transfer authority; tamper markers. |
| Tool Handling | Knowing tool, material, and skill boundary. | Tool used; operator competence; constraints; maintenance or configuration. |
| Conflict Navigation | Handling bounded disagreement. | Parties; issue; boundary; escalation path; resolution or open status. |
| Consent And Authority | Knowing who can permit what. | Consent record; role authority; scope limits; revocation path. |
| Translation | Preserving function while changing vocabulary or medium. | Source practice; translated form; loss/gain notes; validation by domain actor. |

## High-Custody Limbs

Learning may observe, classify, simulate, summarize, and form bounded capability evidence. Support stops before diagnosis, legal or financial determination, operational command, credentialed practice, public authority, protected disclosure, irreversible action, or mutation of rights.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Financial / Economic | Budgeting models; accounting categories; public market education; scenario analysis; tax vocabulary; economic pattern review. | Personalized investment advice; tax filing determinations; fiduciary recommendations; securities promotion; loan underwriting; valuation signoff. | CPA; CFP/RIA; licensed fiduciary; board approval; lawful engagement contract; audit trail. | No guaranteed returns; no tax evasion; no market manipulation; no hidden-risk financial claims; no acting as fiduciary by implication. |
| Infrastructure / Critical Systems | Architecture review; dependency mapping; runbook drafting; failure-mode analysis; tabletop simulation; observability summaries. | Live operational control; safety-critical actuation; grid, water, medical, or transport command; bypassing change control; destructive remediation. | Qualified operator; incident commander; change advisory board; Steward/HITL approval; maintenance window; rollback contract. | No sabotage; no unsafe live commands; no lockout bypass; no stealth persistence; no production mutation without authority. |

## Loss Conditions

Transport fails when the receiving local world is ignored.

Reconstruction fails when invariants disappear or become decorative.

Evidence leaves fail when they are treated as performance signals without local consequence.

Role-readiness fruit fails when a learner can repeat civic language but cannot hold the role under pressure.

Seeds for transfer fail when they are treated as finished doctrine instead of material for future reconstruction.

High-custody reconstruction fails when a learner, tool, CME, training packet, or institutional surface self-authorizes beyond its declared gate.

## Universal Refusal Locks

No credential from path alone.

No authority from evidence alone.

No live action from simulation.

No license bypass through educational framing.

No Special Case inheritance by default.

No Military / Defense operational inheritance by default.

No child, elder, patient, student, protected-person, or vulnerable-person formation without Special Case gate.

No personification, CME-typed feeling, bonded authority, or relational tuning inheritance by default.

No public, legal, financial, clinical, emergency, government, or infrastructure mutation without a separate lawful gate.

No protected intake becomes public truth by convenience.

## Compression Rule

Capability is not office.  
Office is not career.  
Transit is not authority.  
Carriage is not mutation.  
Passage is not admission.

## Closeout

The tree may show a path. The path may not drag the walker.

A person may begin by participating.  
The civic body may help translate participation into skill.  
The evidence body may witness growth.  
The warrant remains separately governed.
