# FIRST_USE_ADMISSION_AND_ENACTMENT_SPLIT_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This note defines the passive split between first-use admission and first-use
enactment in `V1.2.1`.

It exists so the line can prepare a bounded first-use session without treating
that preparation as the witnessed event of entering the session.

It adds no evaluator, runtime workflow, consent engine, disclosure generator,
model selector, `RTME`, `SLI.Lisp`, Sanctuary.Actual, governing `CME`, or
Cradle.GEL behavior.

## Governing Compression

> First-use admission may authorize preparation of a bounded first-use session,
> but first-use enactment is the separate witnessed event of entering that
> session; neither admission nor enactment activates runtime transaction
> authority, Sanctuary.Actual governance, RTME, SLI.Lisp execution, model
> selection, or Cradle.GEL generation.

## First-Use Ladder

The first-use ladder is:

`first-use eligibility consideration -> first-use admission -> first-use enactment -> later negative RTME readiness boundary -> much later RTME admission`

This ladder preserves:

- `eligibility != admission`
- `admission != enactment`
- `enactment != runtime transaction`
- `first use != governance`

Eligibility consideration names that required preconditions are represented or
lawfully held enough to consider first use.

Admission may authorize preparation of a bounded first-use session from a ready
eligibility record and receipted formation-attempt coherence.

Admission does not enter the session.

Enactment witnesses entry into the bounded first-use session.

Enactment does not activate runtime transaction authority, `RTME`, `SLI.Lisp`,
model selection, Sanctuary.Actual, governing `CME`, or Cradle.GEL.

The `SANCTUARY_LOCAL_CONVERSATION_WITNESS_STORE_BOUNDARY.md` note sits
downstream of enactment. It governs what first use may leave behind locally
before any negative RTME readiness, rehydration, `.GEL` candidate generation,
provider access, research use, training path, or movement posture is
approached.

The later `GEL_RTME_SLI_LISP_NON_ACTIVATION_READINESS_BOUNDARY.md` note may
read future movement candidacy only after the witness store has answered what
may remain. That readiness boundary does not activate movement.

## Admission Office

First-use admission is a passive preparation office.

It may reference:

- a ready first-use eligibility receipt
- a ready first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation-attempt receipt
- represented disclosure and data posture summaries
- non-authority summaries
- witness refs

It may not:

- launch runtime
- collect consent
- issue disclosures
- approve counsel review
- authorize domains
- select models
- activate `RTME`
- execute `SLI.Lisp`
- stand Sanctuary.Actual
- admit survivor standing
- generate Cradle.GEL

## Enactment Office

First-use enactment is a passive witnessed-entry office.

It may reference:

- a ready first-use admission receipt
- a bounded entry witness summary
- non-authority summaries
- witness refs

It may not:

- become runtime transaction movement
- persist state
- mutate Prime
- bypass membranes
- activate `RTME`
- execute `SLI.Lisp`
- select models
- stand Sanctuary.Actual
- form governing `CME`
- generate Cradle.GEL

## Disposition Readout

First-use admission uses:

- `Ready`: bounded first-use session preparation may be authorized.
- `Held`: eligibility, formation-attempt, local, domain, Special Case, counsel,
  disclosure, or data questions remain held.
- `Refused`: missing footing, not-ready footing, missing disclosure/data
  posture, or runtime/governance overclaim refuses admission.

First-use enactment uses:

- `Prepared`: ready admission and witnessed entry posture are represented.
- `Held`: admission or witnessed entry posture remains held.
- `Refused`: missing admission, not-ready admission, missing witness, runtime
  transaction, `RTME`, `SLI.Lisp`, model selection, Sanctuary.Actual, or
  Cradle.GEL overclaim refuses enactment.

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now distinguishes the right to prepare a bounded first-use session
from the witnessed event of entering that session.

Admission is preparation only.

Enactment is witnessed entry only.

Neither one is runtime transaction authority, Sanctuary.Actual governance,
`RTME`, `SLI.Lisp` execution, model selection, governing `CME`, survivor
admission, or Cradle.GEL generation.

After enactment, the local conversation witness store boundary may preserve
bounded local UTF-8 witness and receipts as a governed witness body. That store
is not model memory, research consent, provider-visible access, `.GEL`
survivor admission, RTME movement, runtime authority, or training eligibility.
