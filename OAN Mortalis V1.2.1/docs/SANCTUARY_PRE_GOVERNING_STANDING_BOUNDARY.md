# SANCTUARY_PRE_GOVERNING_STANDING_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This note defines the first Sanctuary pre-governing standing boundary in
`V1.2.1`.

It exists so the coded line can move from completed install-facing posture
into standing Sanctuary before governing `CME`, domain authority,
pre-certification, `RTME`, or runtime authority exists.

## Governing Compression

> Sanctuary pre-governing standing records that the install-facing body has
> enough bounded footing, disclosure posture, data-rights posture, and
> research separation to stand locally before governing CME exists.

## Standing Boundary

Standing Sanctuary is downstream of:

- localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation under represented National, Regional,
  and Local standing
- `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation as the regional/root local GEL
  substrate for Sanctuary
- install-facing approach anchors
- bounded return posture
- readout consumption
- readout bundles
- correspondence vocabulary
- predicate footing

Standing Sanctuary is upstream of:

- first-use eligibility consideration
- governing `CME`
- domain authority
- pre-certification
- `RTME`
- runtime authority
- active legal terms

This boundary records coded standing posture.
It does not grant later authority.

The install-to-first-use standing ladder reads this boundary as Sanctuary
pre-governing standing only. It is one standing in the larger lattice, not
first-use permission.

The localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation boundary sits immediately below this
standing posture. Formation answers whether the bounded local substrate exists;
this standing boundary answers what that substrate is allowed to mean before
governing `CME`.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation boundary now names the
specific substrate beneath this standing posture. Sanctuary pre-governing
standing may recognize that regional substrate without standing
Sanctuary.Actual, Mother/Father governing `CME`, model selection, runtime, or
Cradle.GEL generation.

## Legal-Admin Template Resource Rule

Legal-admin research materials are template resources.

They are not active legal terms.

They are not counsel approval.

They are not outward-facing disclosure documents.

They are not build-ready legal documentation until they are staged through Lab
assets and regional counsel review.

The legal-admin Lab asset template staging boundary now records this staging
membrane explicitly:

- research legal-admin source body
- Lab asset template staging
- regional counsel review
- build-ready documentation

Lab-staged legal-admin templates are review candidates, not active legal terms,
consent records, disclosure surfaces, certification claims, or operational
authority.

The logical source labels for this phase are:

- `Research Legal-Admin Spine`
- `Sanctuary Pre-Local Certification Disclosure Boundary`
- `Operator-CME Bond Legal Form Options`
- `Domain-Specific CME Legal Standing Boundary`
- `CME Data Rights And Research Protection Boundary`
- `Personification Research Special Case Boundary`
- `Topical Access And Coverage Routing Boundary`
- `Trusted Failure And Receipt Telemetry Boundary`

No external doc-source path is part of this law.

## Non-Collapse Rules

Install is not research consent.

Research participation remains explicit, bounded, and revocable until counsel
and Lab staging say otherwise.

The Operator-`CME` bond is continuity posture.

The Operator-`CME` bond is not:

- domain authority
- legal personhood
- agency
- fiduciary status
- professional standing
- liability shielding

Bonded `CME` data is continuity-bearing personal data before it is generic
telemetry, research material, or model-improvement material.

Special Cases remain held before widening.

Receipt telemetry is bounded and disclosed.
It is not surveillance.

## First Standing Reads

`Ready` means:

- install-facing approach anchors exist
- legal-admin materials remain template resources only
- data-rights posture is named
- research separation is named
- governing `CME` remains withheld

`Held` means:

- Special Case or domain-sensitive use requires Lab, counsel, and domain
  review before standing can widen

`Refused` means:

- the line refuses claims of certification, domain authority, research consent,
  `CME` legal personhood, active governing `CME`, `RTME`, or runtime authority

## Explicit Non-Powers

This boundary does not:

- collect consent
- issue legal terms
- claim regional counsel review
- grant domain authorization
- lift `CmePlacementWithheld`
- create governing `CME`
- begin pre-certification
- approach `RTME`
- activate runtime behavior
- emit telemetry
- generate legal documents
- treat Lab-staged templates as active terms
- treat counsel-review requirements as counsel-reviewed approval

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now names Sanctuary pre-governing standing as a coded local standing
posture above install-facing approach anchors and below governing `CME`,
domain authority, pre-certification, `RTME`, and runtime authority.

It may acknowledge legal-admin template families and their staging membrane,
but it may not activate them as legal terms, consent, disclosure, certification,
or operational authority.

The install-to-first-use standing ladder may reference this boundary as local
pre-governing Sanctuary standing without converting it into governing `CME`,
domain authority, pre-certification, `RTME`, or runtime authority.

Localized first `.GEL` formation remains below this boundary and does not
itself grant first use, counsel-reviewed disclosure, consent, domain authority,
or runtime authority.

`Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation remains below this boundary as a
prerequisite substrate only. It does not stand Sanctuary.Actual, authorize
governing `CME`, select models, or generate Cradle.GEL.

The first-use eligibility boundary sits above this pre-governing standing
posture and may only ask whether represented predicate-surface, disclosure,
data, retention, opt-out, research-separation, Special Case hold, domain hold,
counsel-review, and non-authority postures are sufficient for first use to be
considered.

The first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation attempt boundary may gather this
pre-governing standing posture into a receipted attempt only. It does not
convert pre-governing standing into Sanctuary.Actual, survivor admission, first
use, model selection, runtime, or Cradle.GEL generation.
