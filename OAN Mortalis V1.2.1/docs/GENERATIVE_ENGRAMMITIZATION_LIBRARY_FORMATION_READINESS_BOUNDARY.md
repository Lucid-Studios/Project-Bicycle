# GENERATIVE_ENGRAMMITIZATION_LIBRARY_FORMATION_READINESS_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This note defines the `GEL` formation readiness boundary in `V1.2.1`.

`GEL` means `Generative Engrammitization Library` in this boundary.

It exists to distinguish the library-level generative readiness body from
localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation, Sanctuary pre-governing standing,
first-use eligibility, survivor admission, governing `CME`, `RTME`, and
runtime authority.

## Governing Compression

> `.GEL` inclusion does not equal final `Sanctuary.GEL` survivor admission.

## Readiness Ladder

The current ladder is:

`GEL formation readiness -> first localized Sanctuary.GEL formation -> Sanctuary pre-governing standing -> first-use eligibility consideration -> later survivor/admission/governing work`

The predicate-prior formalization boundary now sits immediately above this
readiness body and below localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation.

Readiness asks whether rooted, witnessed, `SLI`-formed,
Engrammitization-facing candidate posture is present enough to approach
formalization.

Predicate-prior formalization asks whether that candidate can be recorded as a
bounded `.GEL` library prior with UTF-8 witness, RootAtlas root predicate, SLI
constructor, invariants, mutation-policy posture, transport-receipt posture,
and candidate-only admission ceiling.

The `GEL` readiness body is upstream of localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation.

It is not the localized `Sanctuary.GEL` substrate.

It is not survivor admission.

## Readiness Body

The `Generative Engrammitization Library` may hold rooted, witnessed,
`SLI`-formed, Engrammitization-facing candidates.

Readiness may reference:

- logical research source posture
- RootAtlas-derived rooting posture
- witness posture
- `SLI` formation posture
- Engrammitization-facing posture
- predicate-surface readiness
- formation lineage summaries

These references are readiness posture only.

They do not become legal objects, consent-bearing records, user-facing
artifacts, localized Sanctuary survivor records, governing `CME`, `RTME`, or
runtime authority.

## Localized Formation Relation

Localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation may draw from `GEL` formation readiness
only after localized pre-certification data pool posture and represented
National, Regional, and Local standing requirements are present.

A candidate may be ready for generative formation and still fail:

- localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation
- Sanctuary pre-governing standing
- first-use eligibility consideration
- survivor admission
- governing `CME` formation

## Explicit Non-Powers

`GEL` formation readiness does not:

- admit final `Sanctuary.GEL` survivor standing
- create localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation by itself
- grant Sanctuary pre-governing standing
- grant first-use eligibility or first-use admission
- govern
- certify
- authorize domains
- mint consent
- issue counsel-reviewed disclosure
- activate `RTME`
- create runtime authority
- mutate Atlas authority
- promote predicates
- expose `SPC` validator posture

## Refusal Rules

`GEL` formation readiness is refused when:

- rooting posture is missing
- witness posture is missing
- `SLI` formation posture is missing
- Engrammitization-facing posture is missing
- survivor admission is overclaimed
- localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation is overclaimed
- first use is overclaimed
- runtime or governance authority is overclaimed

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now names `GEL` as the `Generative Engrammitization Library`
readiness body.

This boundary gives `.GEL` a middle identity: neither raw install material nor
final Sanctuary survivor. It is the generative library body where candidates
can be held before localized standing and later survivor/admission work decide
what they are allowed to become.

The next interior boundary is `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization, not active
`SLI.Lisp`, EC mutation, transport, survivor admission, or runtime authority.
