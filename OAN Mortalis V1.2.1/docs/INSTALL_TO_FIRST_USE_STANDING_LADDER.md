# INSTALL_TO_FIRST_USE_STANDING_LADDER

## Purpose

This note defines the install-to-first-use standing ladder in `V1.2.1`.

It exists to read the current build as a lattice of lawful standings rather
than as one readiness gate.

## Governing Compression

> install-to-first-use standing is a lattice of bounded standings; no single
> standing grants first use, runtime authority, research consent, domain
> authority, active legal terms, or governing CME.

## Standing Lattice

The current install-to-first-use lattice contains seven bounded standings.

Install assent standing records localized operator assent footing.
It is not research consent, domain authority, legal-term issuance, or runtime
permission.

`.GEL` formation footing standing records bounded formation data, predicate
pool, first predicate-family footing, and represented National, Regional, and
Local standing.
It is computational footing, not bonded cognition validation or governing
`CME`.

The localized pre-certification data pool now names the represented
source/template/standing input body beneath localized `.GEL` formation.

The `GEL` formation readiness body now names the `Generative
Engrammitization Library` candidate posture beneath localized `.GEL`
formation. `.GEL` inclusion does not equal final `Sanctuary.GEL` survivor
admission.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization body now names the structured
candidate-only prior beneath localized `.GEL` formation. It records UTF-8
witness, RootAtlas root predicate, `SLI` constructor, invariants,
mutation-policy posture, transport-receipt posture, and candidate-only
admission ceiling without granting first use or survivor admission.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation body now names the specific
regional/root local GEL substrate for Sanctuary beneath pre-governing standing.
It is prerequisite to later Sanctuary.Actual Mother/Father governing `CME`
consideration and later Cradle.GEL generation, but it grants neither.

Install-facing expression standing records correspondence vocabulary and
bounded outward readout.
It is operator-facing legibility, not disclosure activation, policy issuance,
or use permission.

Readout reception and return standing records bounded consumption, retention,
deferral, refusal closure, and witnessed forward horizon.
It is local consequence, not `RTME` handoff or runtime activation.

Approach anchor standing records future control-surface,
`SLI.Lisp` telemetry-anchor, and predicate-template-anchor eligibility.
It is future-facing anchor posture, not active telemetry, template generation,
pre-certification, or handoff.

Sanctuary pre-governing standing records that the line may stand locally from
bounded footing, disclosure posture, data-rights posture, and research
separation before governing `CME` exists.
It is local standing, not domain authority, active legal terms, or runtime
authority.

Legal-admin Lab asset template staging standing records legal-admin template
families as review candidates.
It is staging posture, not legal terms, consent records, disclosure surfaces,
certification claims, or operational authority.

## Standing Kinds

Computational standing names what is typed, formed, witnessed, receipted, or
referenced.

Legal-admin standing names disclosure, data-rights, retention, counsel-review,
and consent-separation posture.

Operator-facing standing names correspondence, readout, reception, and bounded
return.

Research standing remains separated from install and consent until explicit
research participation is lawfully admitted.

Domain standing remains held unless separately admitted, counsel-reviewed,
site-bound, role-bound, and revocable.

Runtime standing remains withheld.

These kinds may support one another, but they are not equivalent.

## First-Use Eligibility Horizon

The next membrane above this map is `FIRST_USE_ELIGIBILITY_BOUNDARY.md`.

That later membrane may define what must be represented before first use may be
considered.

That membrane now names predicate-surface readiness, disclosure posture, local
data posture, retention posture, opt-out posture, research separation, Special
Case hold, domain hold, counsel-review state, and non-authority posture as
required before first use may be considered.

First use may be considered is future eligibility language, not permission.

Before that future membrane, the localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation boundary
must keep first `.GEL` formation below Sanctuary pre-governing standing and
grounded in represented National, Regional, and Local standing.

The localized pre-certification data pool must keep Lab asset, RootAtlas
regional posture, legal-admin template-family, data-rights, research, Special
Case, domain, and non-authority inputs representational rather than active.

The `GEL` formation readiness body must keep rooted, witnessed, `SLI`-formed,
Engrammitization-facing candidates in library readiness rather than survivor
admission, first-use permission, governance, `RTME`, or runtime.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization body must keep structured priors
non-mutational, non-transporting, non-runtime, non-governing, and
candidate-only.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation body must keep the formed
regional substrate distinct from Sanctuary.Actual, model selection, active
governance, runtime, and Cradle.GEL generation.

## Explicit Non-Powers

This ladder does not:

- grant first use
- activate runtime behavior
- collect research consent
- issue legal terms
- activate disclosure surfaces
- authorize domain standing
- claim regional counsel review
- generate legal documents
- emit telemetry
- approach `RTME`
- begin pre-certification
- create governing `CME`
- copy legal-admin template bodies into Build

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now names install-to-first-use as a standing lattice.

The ladder lets Build read operator assent, computational formation,
operator-facing expression, return posture, approach anchors, pre-governing
Sanctuary standing, and legal-admin staging together without allowing any one
standing to become first-use permission.

The localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation boundary now clarifies that formation
answers whether the bounded local substrate exists, while standing answers
what the formed substrate is allowed to mean.

The first-use eligibility boundary now clarifies that even localized formation
and pre-governing standing do not grant first use.

The localized pre-certification data pool now clarifies what represented inputs
may contextualize localized formation before that point.

The `GEL` formation readiness body now clarifies what generative library
candidate posture may contextualize localized formation before survivor or
admission work.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization body now clarifies what structured
library prior may contextualize localized formation before active `SLI.Lisp`,
EC mutation, transport, survivor admission, or first use.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation body now clarifies the
substrate lineage: `GEL prior -> Sanctuary.GEL regional substrate -> later
Sanctuary.Actual governance -> later Cradle.GEL generation`.
