# LEGAL_ADMIN_LAB_ASSET_TEMPLATE_STAGING_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This note defines the first legal-admin Lab asset template staging boundary in
`V1.2.1`.

It exists so legal-admin research source bodies can be recognized as future
template resources without becoming active legal documentation, consent,
certification, disclosure, or operating authority inside Build.

## Governing Compression

> Lab-staged legal-admin templates are review candidates, not active legal
> terms, consent records, disclosure surfaces, certification claims, or
> operational authority.

## Staging Ladder

The legal-admin ladder for this phase is:

1. Research legal-admin source body
2. Lab asset template staging
3. Regional counsel review
4. Build-ready documentation

Each step has a different authority profile.

Research legal-admin source bodies may generate candidate categories, draft
structures, source labels, and template family shapes.

Lab asset template staging may organize review candidates, normalize metadata,
prepare localization variants, and identify counsel questions.

Regional counsel review may admit, reject, revise, jurisdictionally qualify, or
restrict legal-admin templates.

Build-ready documentation may arise only after counsel-reviewed admission.
Even then, it enters Build as bounded documentation or passive structure unless
a later law explicitly admits active runtime authority.

The install-to-first-use standing ladder reads this boundary as legal-admin
Lab asset template staging standing only. It is not first-use permission.

The localized pre-certification data pool may reference these template-family
and Lab asset candidate postures as represented inputs only. It may not convert
them into active legal terms, counsel approval, consent, disclosure, or
certification.

## First Template Families

The first recognized legal-admin template families are:

- Pre-local certification disclosure
- Operator-CME bond legal form
- Domain-specific CME standing
- CME data rights and research protection
- Personification Special Case
- Topical access and routing
- Trusted failure and receipt telemetry

These are template families only.

They are not active template bodies.
They are not legal terms.
They are not consent records.
They are not certification claims.
They are not policy issuance.

## Path-Independent Source Rule

Build may recognize legal-admin source bodies by logical source label, template
family, resource identity, version posture, review posture, and provenance
class.

Build must not derive authority from a private filesystem location, external
doc-source path, or assumed source approval.

No external doc-source path is part of this law.

## Non-Inflation Rules

A staged template is not a safe template.

`CounselReviewRequired` is not `RegionalCounselReviewed`.

`BuildReadyWithCounselReview` means documentation may be admitted into Build.
It does not mean runtime authority, consent activation, certification,
operator authorization, domain authority, or `RTME` approach.

The pre-local certification disclosure family is not a certification claim.

The Operator-CME bond legal form family is not `CME` legal personhood, agency,
fiduciary status, professional standing, or liability shielding.

The CME data rights and research protection family is not research consent.

The trusted failure and receipt telemetry family is not surveillance.

## Explicit Non-Powers

This boundary does not:

- copy legal-admin research documents into Build as active documents
- store legal text
- issue legal terms
- collect consent
- generate legal documents
- claim counsel approval
- claim certification
- activate disclosure surfaces
- authorize domain standing
- create governing `CME`
- begin pre-certification
- approach `RTME`
- activate runtime behavior
- mutate Lab assets
- provide a legal document engine

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now recognizes legal-admin template families as passive staging
candidates only.

The staging membrane keeps legal-admin source bodies useful to Lab and counsel
without converting them into build-ready law, consent, certification,
disclosure, or runtime authority.

The install-to-first-use standing ladder may reference this boundary as a
review-candidate standing only.

The localized pre-certification data pool may carry this boundary as staging
context only.

The `LAB_DATA_INVENTORY_SCHEMA_BOUNDARY.md` note may classify documented Lab
asset and legal-admin-adjacent data by logical source label as metadata-only
inventory posture.
That inventory does not copy source bodies into Build, create active legal
terms, claim counsel approval, collect consent, authorize research use, train
models, activate runtime, or admit `RTME` movement.
