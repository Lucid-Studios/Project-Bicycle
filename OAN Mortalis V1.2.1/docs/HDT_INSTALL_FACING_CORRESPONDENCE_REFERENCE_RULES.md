# HDT_INSTALL_FACING_CORRESPONDENCE_REFERENCE_RULES

## Purpose

This note defines how later `HDT` artifact and evidence surfaces may reference
the install-facing correspondence vocabulary seated in `V1.2.1`.

It exists so `HDT` may support inspection and evidence without being mistaken
for runtime, admissibility, or certification authority.

## Governing Compression

> `HDT` may reference install-facing correspondence vocabulary for evidence and
> support.
> `HDT` may not use that reference to claim truth, admissibility,
> certification, runtime standing, or operator authorization.
> install-facing correspondence remains reference-only even when cited by
> `HDT`.

## Allowed HDT Reference Uses

Later `HDT` surfaces may reference install-facing correspondence vocabulary for:

- evidence support
- inspection context
- relation/context surfaces
- comparison/support readout

`HDT` may point to the vocabulary as a bounded contextual aid.

## Forbidden HDT Elevations

`HDT` may not use that reference to claim:

- truth
- admissibility
- certification
- runtime standing
- operator authorization

It may not redefine correspondence vocabulary or elevate it into runtime law.

`V1.2.1` may not treat `HDT` reference as admissibility proof.

## Working Summary

`HDT` may later reference install-facing correspondence vocabulary as a
governed evidence and support lane, but that reference remains contextual only
and does not mint authority.
