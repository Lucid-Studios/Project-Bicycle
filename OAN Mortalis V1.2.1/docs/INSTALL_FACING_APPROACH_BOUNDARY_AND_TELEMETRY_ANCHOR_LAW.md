# INSTALL_FACING_APPROACH_BOUNDARY_AND_TELEMETRY_ANCHOR_LAW

## Purpose

This note defines the first passive approach boundary above the completed
install-facing body in `V1.2.1`.

It exists so bounded return postures can name future control-surface,
`SLI.Lisp` telemetry, and predicate-template anchor eligibility without
becoming active telemetry emission, template generation, pre-certification,
`RTME` approach, `HDT` authority, or runtime behavior.

## Governing Compression

> approach boundary names future eligibility from bounded return posture; it
> does not emit, template, certify, hand off, or activate authority.

## Sequence

The install-facing sequence now reads:

`predicate footing -> correspondence -> readout bundle -> readout consumption -> return posture -> approach boundary`

The approach boundary is downstream of install-facing return posture.

It is upstream of any future pre-certification, `SLI.Lisp` telemetry,
predicate templating, `RTME`, `HDT`, or runtime work.

The next standing boundary above approach anchors is Sanctuary
pre-governing standing. That boundary may record coded local standing before
governing `CME` exists, but it may not convert approach anchors into legal
terms, counsel approval, domain authority, `RTME`, or runtime behavior.

The install-to-first-use standing ladder reads approach anchors as one bounded
standing inside a larger lattice. It does not allow approach anchors to grant
first use or collapse into pre-governing standing.

## Allowed Naming

This boundary may name:

- future control-surface eligibility
- future `SLI.Lisp` telemetry anchor eligibility
- future predicate-template anchor eligibility
- witness references tying those eligibilities back to bounded return posture

The first allowed posture reads are:

- `Retained` may become future local retention control-surface eligibility
- `Deferred` may become future deferred-hold control-surface eligibility
- `ClosedRefusal` may become future refusal-closure control-surface eligibility
- `WitnessedForwardHorizon` may become future forward-horizon control-surface,
  telemetry-anchor, and predicate-template-anchor eligibility

## Forward Horizon Rule

`WitnessedForwardHorizon` is future-facing only.

It may be carried as a future `SLI.Lisp` telemetry anchor.

It may be carried as a future predicate-template anchor.

It may not emit telemetry, generate templates, approach `RTME`, begin
pre-certification, or act as a handoff.

## Explicit Non-Powers

This boundary does not:

- emit telemetry
- generate templates
- issue predicate templates
- mutate `.lisp` files
- create `SLI.Lisp` services
- certify or admit authority
- hand off authority
- approach `RTME`
- begin pre-certification
- invoke `HDT`
- activate runtime behavior

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now names the first approach boundary above install-facing return
posture as passive eligibility only. It lets future control surfaces,
`SLI.Lisp` telemetry points, and predicate templates line up with bounded
return posture without causing those future layers to exist yet.

The install-to-first-use standing ladder may reference this boundary as
approach anchor standing only.
