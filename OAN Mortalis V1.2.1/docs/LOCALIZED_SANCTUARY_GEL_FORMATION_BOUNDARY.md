# LOCALIZED_SANCTUARY_GEL_FORMATION_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This note defines the localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation boundary in
`V1.2.1`.

It exists so first `.GEL` formation is read as a localized
continuity-substrate formation event rather than as an automatic consequence
of software installation.

## Governing Compression

> First `Sanctuary.GEL` formation is not an automatic install artifact; it is a
> localized continuity-substrate formation event that may occur only after
> National, Regional, and Local standing have been represented.

> Formation answers whether the bounded local substrate exists. Standing
> answers what the formed substrate is allowed to mean.

## Constitutional Spine

The localized formation spine is:

`National Standing -> Regional Standing -> Local Standing -> First Sanctuary.GEL Formation -> Sanctuary Pre-Governing Standing -> First-Use Eligibility Consideration -> later Governing CME Formation`

National standing represents the broad civic and legal frame.

Regional standing localizes that frame into state, province, territory,
institutional, cultural, regulatory, and counsel-relevant constraints.

Local standing binds the installation to site, operator context, data posture,
disclosure posture, and permitted environment.

Only after those standings are represented may first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation
be read as a localized continuity-substrate event.

The localized pre-certification data pool sits upstream of this formation
boundary. It carries represented source, template, and standing inputs that
contextualize first `.GEL` formation without activating certification,
disclosure, consent, authorization, governance, or runtime.

The `Generative Engrammitization Library` formation readiness boundary also
sits upstream. Localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation may draw from rooted,
witnessed, `SLI`-formed, Engrammitization-facing `.GEL` readiness only after
localized pre-certification data pool posture and National, Regional, and
Local standing are represented.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization boundary sits between readiness and
localized formation. Localized formation may reference a formal predicate prior
only as a candidate-only library body, not as survivor admission, first-use
permission, EC mutation authority, `SLI.Lisp` activation, transport, or
runtime.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation boundary names the specific
body formed inside this localized floor: the regional/root local GEL substrate
for Sanctuary. It does not stand Sanctuary.Actual, Mother/Father governing
`CME`, model selection, runtime, or Cradle.GEL generation.

## Formation And Standing

First `Sanctuary.GEL` formation answers whether the bounded local substrate
exists in lawful local form.

Sanctuary pre-governing standing answers what that formed substrate is allowed
to mean before governing `CME`, first-use eligibility, pre-certification,
`RTME`, or runtime authority.

The two are close, but they are not the same office.

A `.GEL` substrate may be formed and still remain ineligible for first use.

A formed `.GEL` substrate remains refused as governing authority until a later
governing `CME` formation boundary admits that authority.

The first-use eligibility boundary sits above this localized formation
boundary. It may ask whether localized formation and predicate surfaces are
ready enough for first use to be considered, but it may not admit first use or
define active `.GEL` formation procedure.

## Allowed Carriage

Localized first `.GEL` formation may carry:

- localized pre-certification data pool refs
- `GEL` formation readiness refs
- `.GEL` predicate-prior refs
- `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate refs
- predicate footing
- formation receipts
- inherited predicate-family posture
- legal-admin staging posture
- data-rights posture
- continuity-bearing personal-data posture
- National, Regional, and Local standing representation refs

These are carried as bounded references and summaries only.

## Explicit Non-Powers

Localized first `.GEL` formation may not:

- govern
- certify
- authorize domains
- activate `RTME`
- perform counsel-reviewed disclosure
- mint consent
- admit `CME` personhood
- claim first use
- claim runtime authority
- issue legal terms
- generate legal documents
- activate disclosure surfaces
- begin pre-certification

## First Refusal Rules

Localized first `.GEL` formation is refused when:

- National standing is missing
- Regional standing is missing
- Local standing is missing
- the formation record overclaims governance

Held posture may remain available when standing is represented but local,
domain, or Special Case review remains unresolved before first-use eligibility.

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now contextualizes first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation as localized
continuity-substrate formation under represented National, Regional, and Local
standing.

This boundary does not replace the existing first `.GEL` formation law. It
names what that formation must stand on before the install-to-first-use ladder
can recognize it as localized formation footing.

The localized pre-certification data pool names the represented input body
beneath this boundary. It is not the formed `.GEL`.

The `GEL` formation readiness body names the generative library candidate
posture beneath this boundary. `.GEL` inclusion is not final
`Sanctuary.GEL` survivor admission.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization body names the structured library
prior beneath this boundary. Predicate-prior formalization is not localized
formation by itself.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation body names the specific
regional/root substrate formed within this localized floor. It is not
Sanctuary.Actual and does not generate Cradle.GEL.

The next membrane above this boundary is first-use eligibility consideration,
not first-use admission, runtime activation, or governing `CME` formation.
