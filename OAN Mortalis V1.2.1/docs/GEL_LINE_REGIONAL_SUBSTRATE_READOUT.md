# GEL_LINE_REGIONAL_SUBSTRATE_READOUT

## Purpose

This readout consolidates the current `.GEL` line in `V1.2.1`.

It adds no new authority, service, evaluator, runtime, admission gate, or
formation owner.

It exists to keep the freshly separated meanings visible before the line moves
upward into either deeper first-use enactment consequences or
Sanctuary.Actual approach work.

## Governing Compression

> `Sanctuary.GEL` is a prerequisite regional substrate for later
> Sanctuary.Actual and Cradle.GEL work; it is not itself a governing actor,
> runtime body, model-selection surface, or CradleTek-local GEL.

It preserves source text, encoding state, token or span bounds, local context,
source witness, ambiguity, and Unicode snapshot posture before symbolic
assignment.
Symbolics may compress and deepen the object, but they may not replace the
witness.

It does not create local RootAtlas authority, Atlas mutation, domain authority,
or universal source sovereignty.

`SLI` constructors are structured and attachment-native.
They are carried as `prefix.super`, `prefix.sub`, `body`, `suffix.super`, and
`suffix.sub`.
A flat rendering is display-only and is not canonical authority.

`GEL = Generative Engrammitization Library` readiness may hold rooted,
witnessed, `SLI`-formed, Engrammitization-facing candidates.
Readiness is not localized `Sanctuary.GEL` formation and not survivor
admission.

native `SLI` constructor, invariants, mutation-policy posture,
transport-receipt posture, and a candidate-only admission ceiling.

admission, governance, `RTME`, or runtime.

non-authorizing.
It may reference, stage, and localize Lab asset, RootAtlas regional posture,
legal-admin template-family, standing, data-rights, research-separation,
Special Case, domain, and non-authority inputs.
It does not certify, disclose, consent, authorize, govern, or activate
runtime.

and research separation can stand locally before governing `CME`, domain
