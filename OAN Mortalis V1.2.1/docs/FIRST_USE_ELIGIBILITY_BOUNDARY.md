# FIRST_USE_ELIGIBILITY_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This note defines the first-use eligibility boundary in `V1.2.1`.

It exists so Build can name the passive conditions required before first use
may be considered without granting first use, launching runtime, activating
governance, collecting consent, issuing disclosures, or authorizing domains.

## Governing Compression

> First use may be considered only after a localized `Sanctuary.GEL` substrate
> has formed, Sanctuary pre-governing standing has been admitted, and required
> predicate-surface, disclosure, data, retention, opt-out,
> research-separation, Special Case hold, domain hold, counsel-review, and
> non-authority postures have been represented.

## Eligibility Ladder

The current ladder is:

`materials available -> localized substrate formed -> pre-governing standing admitted -> first-use eligibility may be considered -> later first-use admission -> later governing CME formation`

The localized pre-certification data pool sits beneath localized substrate
formation as represented source, template, and standing input context.

The `GEL` formation readiness body sits beneath localized substrate formation
as generative library candidate posture. `.GEL` inclusion does not equal final
`Sanctuary.GEL` survivor admission.

The `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization body sits beneath localized substrate
formation as structured candidate-only library prior. Predicate-prior
formalization does not grant first-use permission.

The `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation body sits beneath this
eligibility membrane as the regional/root local GEL substrate for Sanctuary.
It is prerequisite context, not first-use admission, Sanctuary.Actual, model
selection, or Cradle.GEL generation.

Eligibility consideration is not first-use admission.

Eligibility consideration is not runtime activation.

Eligibility consideration is not governing `CME` formation.

## Predicate Surface Readiness

Predicate-surface readiness is required before first-use eligibility may be
considered.

In this phase predicate-surface readiness means:

- the `Sanctuary.GEL` predicate pool is `Ready`
- posture, trust/authorization, evidence footing, and response/disposition
  predicate families are present
- first `.GEL` substrate records family-bearing predicate inheritance
- explicit `SPC` remains withheld
- validator exposure remains withheld
- predicate promotion remains withheld
- Atlas mutation remains withheld
- runtime reasoning remains withheld

Missing predicate-surface readiness refuses first-use eligibility
consideration.

## Required Legal-Admin And Data Postures

First-use eligibility consideration also requires represented or lawfully held
postures for:

- disclosure posture
- local data posture
- retention posture
- opt-out posture
- research separation
- Special Case hold
- domain-sensitive use hold
- counsel-review state
- non-authority posture

Research separation must be explicit.

Special Cases must remain held.

Domain-sensitive uses must remain held.

Counsel-review state may be represented, but counsel review must not be
overclaimed.

## Formation Process Boundary

This boundary may ask whether predicate surfaces and localized formation are
ready enough to consider first use.

It does not define active `.GEL` formation procedure beyond the already seated
passive formation law.

Active `.GEL` formation-process elaboration remains a later separate boundary.

It also does not turn the localized pre-certification data pool into
certification, disclosure, consent, authorization, governance, or runtime.

It also does not turn `GEL` formation readiness into survivor admission,
localized formation by itself, first-use admission, governing `CME`, `RTME`,
or runtime authority.

It also does not turn `.GEL` predicate-prior formalization into EC mutation,
`SLI.Lisp` activation, transport, final survivor admission, or runtime
authority.

It also does not turn `Sanctuary.GEL` regional substrate formation into
Sanctuary.Actual, Mother/Father governing `CME`, model selection, runtime, or
Cradle.GEL generation.

The first `Sanctuary.GEL` formation attempt boundary sits above eligibility
consideration as a receipted assessment surface. It may gather the eligibility
record into an attempt, but it does not turn eligibility into first-use
admission, enactment, runtime, model selection, Sanctuary.Actual, or
Cradle.GEL generation.

The first formation-attempt readout may report coherence above eligibility.
That readout does not convert eligibility or coherence into first-use
admission, enactment, runtime, Sanctuary.Actual governance, or Cradle.GEL
generation.

The first-use admission and enactment split boundary sits above this
eligibility membrane. Admission may prepare a bounded first-use session, and
enactment may witness entry into that session, but eligibility does not create
admission and admission does not create enactment.

## Refusal Rules

First-use eligibility consideration is refused when:

- localized `.GEL` formation is missing
- predicate-surface readiness is missing
- disclosure posture is missing
- local data posture is missing
- retention or opt-out posture is missing
- Special Cases are not held
- domain-sensitive uses are not held
- research separation is missing
- counsel-review state is overclaimed
- runtime or governing authority is overclaimed

## Explicit Non-Powers

This boundary does not:

- admit first use
- launch runtime
- activate governance
- collect consent
- issue disclosures
- generate legal documents
- approve counsel review
- authorize domains
- activate `RTME`
- begin pre-certification
- create governing `CME`
- expose `SPC`
- mutate Atlas authority
- promote predicates

## Working Summary

`V1.2.1` now names first-use eligibility consideration as a passive membrane
above localized `.GEL` formation and Sanctuary pre-governing standing.

The membrane requires predicate-surface readiness and legal-admin/data posture
representation before first use may be considered, while still refusing first
use permission, runtime, governance, domain authority, counsel approval, and
`RTME`.
