# Civic Education Domain Job Template Matrix

Status: review-only corpus seed.  
Claim ceiling: educational manifold planning.  
Activation status: non-executive, non-credentialing, non-authorizing.

This matrix defines a first civic-standard domain and job-template body for future educational corpus formation. Its purpose is to help translate talents, skills, and abilities into training lanes and witnessed participation paths without making upper-class credential access the first gate.

## Matrix Law

Domain grants context.  
Role grants posture.  
Training grants practice.  
Receipt grants review.  
None of these grant authority by themselves.

Education is not the gate to participation. Education is the engine that keeps participation growing.

Participation is not coercion. Training is not capture. Attunement is not authority. Support is not debt. Evidence is not class gatekeeping.

## Control Legend

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Standard | Ordinary civic training and participation lane. |
| Steward | Requires accountable custody, access control, audit trail, or institutional/lab steward. |
| Licensed | Requires licensed, professionally admitted, statutory, or regulated authority before acting beyond support. |
| HITL | Requires human review before publication, escalation, deployment, or external effect. |
| Special Case | Requires explicit higher-gate review because the work can shape identity, safety, children, vulnerable persons, clinical/legal/financial outcomes, surveillance, force, or long-term formation. |
| Closed | Closed to general template inheritance. |

## Inheritance Locks

Industrial templates do not inherit Civic.  
Civic templates do not inherit Domestic.  
Domestic templates do not inherit Pedagogical.  
Pedagogical does not inherit Clinical.  
Security does not inherit Military.  
Personification and CME-typed feeling do not inherit anywhere by default.

## Corpus Formation Use

This matrix can seed:

- talent-to-skill translation maps;
- skill-to-training packet generators;
- role-readiness gates;
- educational pathway recommendations;
- witnessed practice receipts;
- civic contribution routing;
- domain template pack boundaries;
- Special Case escalation and refusal maps.

It does not certify competence, grant employment eligibility, authorize practice, replace licensed review, or admit operational authority.

## Closeout Law

A person may begin by participating.  
The civic body may help translate participation into skill.  
The evidence body may witness growth.  
The system may not turn uplift into debt, training into capture, or opportunity into a new class gate.
